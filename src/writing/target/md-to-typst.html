After Helix conquered my text editing habits this summer, it demanded complete loyalty. That meant that my note-taking app, brot, built with a Tiptap WYSIWYG editor, needed surgerical alteration to meet the new standards.

I initially tried making an Obsidian-like modal markdown editor to allow myself to edit my notes without changing their representation. However, looking at the strange corners of syntax I had to handle, with useless Setext headings and millions of ways to make bullet points, convinced me to search for articles that confirmed my disapproval of markdown. With my dislike of markdown now supported by credible sources, I decided to convert all my 400 notes to Typst.
